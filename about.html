Our Story

what drives us to strive for the welfare of others

The Gnanam Foundation aims to uplift and empower marginalized communities.

In December 2010, Mr. Allirajah Subaskaran, Chairman of Lycamobile Group, and his wife Mrs. Prema Subaskaran, Chair of Lycahealth, initiated a journey of compassion and service in the name of Mr. Allirajah's mother Gnanambikai Allirajah, in order to provide help for marginalised communities around the world.




Lyca Gnanam Foundation donates 1,350 meals, combating London's cost-of-living crisis through
surplus food rescue with Felix Project

Empowering students through nutrient-rich foods to break the cycle of
poverty and achieve academic success.
